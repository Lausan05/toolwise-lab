AI video generator checklist: what to check before paying

Before subscribing to an AI video generator, test the workflow you actually need. A polished demo does not tell you whether a tool fits your budget, language, publishing format or licensing requirements.

  • Output quality: test the same script across shortlisted tools.
  • Usage limits: check credits, minutes, queues and render restrictions.
  • Watermarks: confirm whether your plan includes watermarked exports.
  • Formats: verify aspect ratios, resolution and file types.
  • Editing: test captions, pronunciation, timing and scene changes.
  • Commercial rights: read the license for your intended use.
  • Languages: test the accents and pronunciation you need.
  • Privacy: understand how uploaded scripts, images and voices are handled.
  • Cancellation: check billing cycles, refunds and unused credits.

Run a fair side-by-side test

Use the same script, audience and output format for every shortlisted product. Record editing time, final quality, export restrictions and cost per finished video.
