How to choose your first AI tools

Start with a task, not a tool

List repetitive tasks that take time every week: drafting product descriptions, preparing social posts, summarizing meetings or organizing leads. Pick one task that is easy to measure.

Define success

Measure time saved, fewer manual steps, faster response time or improved consistency. Do not assume a tool improves a process simply because it uses AI.

Test safely

Start with sample or non-sensitive information. Review privacy, security and data-use terms before uploading confidential business information.

Compare total cost

Check subscription price, usage limits, credits, team seats, export restrictions, taxes and cancellation terms.

Review the output

Check accuracy, quality, editing time, language support and consistency. Human review remains important.

Keep or cancel based on evidence

After a trial, compare the result with your original measure. Keep the subscription only if it solves the problem at a reasonable cost.
