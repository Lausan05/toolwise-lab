How to choose AI tools for a small business

AI tools can help with writing, customer communication, research, design and repetitive admin. The best choice is not necessarily the most popular tool: it is the one that solves a real task at an acceptable cost and risk.

Start with a task, not a tool

List repetitive tasks that take time every week: drafting product descriptions, preparing social posts, summarizing meetings or organizing leads. Pick one task that is easy to measure.

Define success before testing

Measure time saved, fewer manual steps, faster response time or improved consistency. Record the current process so you have a fair baseline.

Test safely and review privacy

Start with sample or non-sensitive information. Review privacy, security and data-use terms before uploading customer details, financial records or confidential business information.

Compare the total cost

Check subscription price, usage limits, credits, team seats, export restrictions, taxes, integrations and cancellation terms. A low starting price may not represent the full cost of your workflow.

Review output quality

Check accuracy, quality, editing time, language support and consistency. Human review remains important, especially for customer-facing or business-critical work.

Keep or cancel based on evidence

After a trial, compare the result with your original measure. Keep the subscription only if it solves the problem at a reasonable cost.
