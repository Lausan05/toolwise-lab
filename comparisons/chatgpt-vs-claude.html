ChatGPT vs Claude: Which AI Assistant Should You Choose?

ChatGPT and Claude are two capable general-purpose AI assistants. Both can help with writing, analysis, research and coding, but their tools, workflows, plan limits and integrations are not identical. The best choice depends on what you need to do—not on a universal winner.

Our approach: this is a practical feature and workflow comparison based on provider information, not a benchmark or hands-on performance test. Features, availability and usage limits change frequently.

Quick answer

  • Choose ChatGPT if you want a broad assistant experience that brings together conversation, image creation, voice, data analysis, custom GPTs and other tools in one product.
  • Choose Claude if its writing and analysis workflow suits you, or you want to use Claude Code as part of a terminal-based coding workflow.
  • Try both free plans with the same real task before paying. Your results will depend on the model available to your account, plan limits and the quality of your instructions.

1. Writing, explanations and everyday work

Both assistants can draft emails, summarize documents, explain concepts, brainstorm ideas and revise text. The difference you notice most may come from response style, how well the assistant follows your instructions and the model selected—not simply the brand.

For an important writing task, test both with the same brief. Compare factual accuracy, tone, structure and how much editing you need to do.

2. Research and working with information

ChatGPT offers web search and deeper research features. Claude also offers web search and research capabilities. Access and limits depend on the plan, account and current product availability. For research that matters, verify important claims against primary sources regardless of which assistant you use.

3. Coding: ChatGPT, Codex, Claude and Claude Code

Both ecosystems support coding assistance, but the workflows are not exactly the same. ChatGPT provides coding-related assistance and Codex access that varies by plan. Claude Code is a terminal-based coding agent that can work with a project codebase. Choose based on your development environment, how much control you want over edits, and whether you prefer an integrated assistant or a terminal workflow.

For a fair test, give both tools the same small task: explain an unfamiliar function, fix a reproducible bug or write a unit test. Review the proposed changes, run the tests and compare correctness—not just how impressive the first answer looks.

4. Images, voice and creative work

ChatGPT includes image-generation and voice capabilities, with usage limits depending on plan. Claude's product includes features such as Artifacts and document or presentation creation. These are different creative workflows, so compare them using the output you actually need rather than treating them as interchangeable features.

5. Free plans and paid pricing

Both products offer a free way to get started, but free usage is limited and the limits can change. Paid plans provide higher usage or additional capabilities. Claude Pro is listed by Anthropic at US$20 per month, or US$200 billed annually (equivalent to about US$17 per month before applicable taxes). ChatGPT offers several individual plan tiers; check its live pricing page for the current price and availability in your country.

Do not choose based on the subscription price alone. Consider how often you use the assistant, which features you need, usage caps, local taxes and whether a paid plan actually saves you time.

6. Privacy and sensitive information

Before uploading documents or source code, review the provider's current data controls and terms for your account type. Avoid sharing passwords, API keys, private customer data or confidential business information unless you have permission and the service's data handling is appropriate for that information.

Final verdict

There is no single winner for every person or task. ChatGPT may be the better starting point if you want a broad set of built-in tools. Claude may be a better fit if its writing and analysis style works for you or Claude Code matches your development workflow. For many users, the most reliable decision is to test both on the same tasks and pay only for the one that consistently improves their work.
